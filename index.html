How it works

A clear process for custom work.

Accurate measurements and approved specifications come before fabrication.

  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    Tell us what you want to replace, install, or design. Photos and rough measurements can help start the conversation.

  2. 02

    On-site measurement

    A technician verifies openings, surfaces, access, and the conditions that affect fit and installation.

  3. 03

    Design and specification

    We confirm glass, product, configuration, finish, performance options, and installation scope.

  4. 04

    Professional fabrication

    Approved custom products are ordered or fabricated to the final specifications.

  5. 05

    Expert installation

    The team protects the work area, removes applicable existing materials, and installs the new product.

  6. 06

    Final inspection and support

    We review operation, appearance, care guidance, and any product-specific warranty information with you.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers help you choose the right next step. Final specifications always follow field measurement and product review.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Contact Elite Glass & Window to discuss the project and schedule a no-obligation consultation or measurement when needed.

Where is your showroom?

The showroom is at 4028 148th Ave NE, Redmond, WA 98052. Contact the team before visiting to confirm current showroom hours.

What areas do you serve?

The team serves Redmond, the Eastside, Seattle, North Seattle and Snohomish County, South King County, and nearby Greater Seattle communities.

Do you handle residential and commercial glass?

Yes. Services include residential windows, doors, showers, mirrors, and railings as well as storefronts, commercial replacement glass, and custom interior glass.

Can you replace only the glass in a window?

Often, yes. If the existing frame is serviceable, replacing a failed insulated glass unit or broken pane can be more efficient than replacing the whole window.

Do you work with contractors and designers?

Yes. Elite Glass & Window works directly with homeowners and also coordinates with contractors, builders, architects, designers, and property managers.

How do I prepare for an estimate?

Share the project address, product or glass type, approximate dimensions, photos, and any timeline constraints. Final custom fabrication still requires verified measurements.
