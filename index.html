How it works

From the first photos to the final walkthrough

Custom work follows a measured sequence so the approved product fits the opening and the written scope.

  1. 1. Share the Problem

    Send photos, the address, approximate dimensions, and what is not working or needs to change.

  2. 2. Compare the Right Options

    The in-house team helps distinguish glass-only repair, full replacement, custom fabrication, and product choices.

  3. 3. Verify and Install

    Final measurements and the approved written scope guide ordering, fabrication, and installation.

  4. 4. Review the Finished Work

    The team completes a walkthrough, with owner involvement in job-site quality checks.

We stand by the quality of our workmanship and the products we install. Warranties vary by product and service; please ask for specific details during your consultation.

Yes, Elite Glass & Windows works on residential and commercial glass and window projects, from home window replacements to storefront installations.
