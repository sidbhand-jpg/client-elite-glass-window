    Other Glass, Window & Door Services
    Can you make a custom glass tabletop?

    Yes. We can help specify dimensions, thickness, edgework, corners, and safety-glass options for the furniture and intended use.

    What glass is available for cabinets and shelves?

    Common choices include clear, frosted, textured, patterned, tempered, and specialty glass. Availability depends on size and application.

    How is tabletop thickness selected?

    Thickness depends on whether the glass protects a supported surface or spans between supports, plus overall size, edge treatment, loading, and safety considerations.

    What measurements are needed for shelves?

    Provide clear opening dimensions, desired depth, support locations, intended load, exposed edges, and any notches or cutouts. Final fabrication follows verified measurements.
