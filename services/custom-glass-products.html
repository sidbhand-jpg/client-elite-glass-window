Custom Glass FAQ

Can you make a glass tabletop to a template?

Yes. Depending on the shape, the team can work from verified dimensions or a physical template and advise on thickness, edgework, corners, and safety glass.

What glass is available for cabinets?

Common choices include clear, frosted, textured, patterned, and specialty glass. Bring a door or accurate opening details to discuss fit and availability.

Do you install glass shelves?

Yes. Shelf dimensions, thickness, supports, loads, and wall conditions are considered before fabrication and installation.
