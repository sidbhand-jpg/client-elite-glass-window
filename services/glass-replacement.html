Glass Replacement FAQ

Can you replace just the glass without replacing the window?

Often, yes. If the sash and frame are in usable condition, the glass or insulated glass unit can frequently be replaced by itself.

Can a foggy double-pane window be repaired?

Fog between panes usually indicates a failed insulated-glass seal. Replacing the sealed glass unit restores clarity and insulation while retaining a suitable frame.

Do you replace tempered and safety glass?

Yes. Tempered, laminated, Low-E, and other specialty glass can be ordered to match the required dimensions and application.
