Doors FAQ

What kinds of exterior doors do you install?

The available catalog includes fiberglass and steel entry doors, decorative-glass doors, sliding and French patio doors, multi-panel systems, and contemporary aluminum folding doors.

Can you replace a patio door with a larger opening?

Potentially. Enlarging an opening can involve structural design, permitting, and coordination with other trades. An on-site consultation is needed before defining that scope.

Do you install interior doors?

The primary focus is exterior entry, patio, and architectural glass door systems. Contact the showroom with the exact interior-door scope to confirm availability.
