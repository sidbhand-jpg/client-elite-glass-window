Installation process

A simple process. Exceptional results.

From first ideas to final inspection, each stage keeps the scope, specifications, and expectations clear.

01

Step 1

Initial consultation

Tell us what you want to replace, install, or design. Photos and rough measurements can help start the conversation.

02

Step 2

On-site measurement

A technician verifies openings, surfaces, access, and the conditions that affect fit and installation.

03

Step 3

Design and specification

We confirm glass, product, configuration, finish, performance options, and installation scope.

04

Step 4

Professional fabrication

Approved custom products are ordered or fabricated to the final specifications.

05

Step 5

Expert installation

The team protects the work area, removes applicable existing materials, and installs the new product.

06

Step 6

Final inspection and support

We review operation, appearance, care guidance, and any product-specific warranty information with you.

Before fabrication

Why final measurements matter

Custom glass cannot be resized after tempering. Finished surfaces, hardware clearances, and verified dimensions are part of getting the fit right.

Do you handle residential and commercial glass?

Yes. Services include residential windows, doors, showers, mirrors, and railings as well as storefronts, commercial replacement glass, and custom interior glass.

Can you replace only the glass in a window?

Often, yes. If the existing frame is serviceable, replacing a failed insulated glass unit or broken pane can be more efficient than replacing the whole window.
