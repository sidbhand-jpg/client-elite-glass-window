Installation Process

A Clear Process from Measurement to Final Walkthrough

Whether you are upgrading windows, installing a custom shower door, or planning a larger residential or commercial project, our team guides you through each stage.

Our Service Process

A Simple Process. Carefully Managed.

At Elite Glass & Windows, we aim to make every project clear, organized, and easy to navigate. The details vary by product and site, but these five stages show what customers can generally expect.

01

On-Site Measurement

We visit the project location and take precise measurements so products can be specified for the actual opening and space.

02

Design & Specification

We review product, glass, hardware, configuration, and finish options, then document the approved direction for your project.

03

Professional Fabrication

Your approved product is ordered or fabricated to the verified measurements and specifications using the appropriate materials.

04

Expert Installation

Our installation team prepares the work area, installs the product carefully, and pays attention to fit, operation, finish, and cleanup.

05

Final Inspection & Support

We review the completed work, confirm operation and finish, and explain relevant care information and follow-up support.

What Helps the Project Move Smoothly

Clear Decisions at Each Stage

Accurate information, documented choices, and realistic scheduling help keep custom work organized.

01

Before Measurement

Share photos, project goals, access considerations, and any known constraints so the visit can focus on the right options.

02

Before Ordering

Review the written scope, product details, glass and hardware selections, measurements, and expected sequence before approval.

03

Before Installation

Confirm access and scheduling details, then move furniture or personal items away from the work area when requested.

Questions Answered

Installation Process FAQs

Answers to common questions about product options, measurements, scheduling, installation, and support.

Do you provide free estimates?

Share photos and project details with the team to discuss an initial estimate and the appropriate next step.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Redmond, the Eastside, Seattle, Snohomish County, South King County, and nearby Greater Seattle communities listed in our service areas.

What shower door styles do you offer?

Options include custom frameless, hinged, and sliding shower-door systems. Available glass, hardware, and configurations depend on the opening and selected product line.

What window brands do you work with?

Current featured window lines include Milgard, Ply Gem, Marvin, Stile, and Sierra Pacific. The team can help compare the options relevant to your project.

How long does a typical door or window installation take?

Timing depends on the verified scope, product availability, access, site conditions, and project complexity. Your written proposal will describe the expected sequence for the specific project.

Do you offer custom-sized doors and windows?

Yes. We provide custom-measured doors, windows, shower glass, railings, storefront systems, and specialty glass solutions based on verified dimensions and approved specifications.

What types of doors do you install?

Projects can include entry doors, patio doors, sliding glass doors, French doors, bi-fold doors, and modern door systems, including options from CODEL and Teza.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Elite Glass & Windows is licensed and insured. Current documentation relevant to your project can be provided on request.

Do you offer financing options?

Availability and terms can change. Contact the team to confirm whether any financing options are currently available for your project.

Can you replace just the glass instead of the entire window?

In many cases, yes. The team can evaluate the existing window and recommend glass replacement or full window replacement based on its condition and your project goals.

Do you work with contractors, builders, and architects?

Yes. We work directly with homeowners and coordinate with contractors, builders, architects, designers, and property managers on residential and commercial projects.

What warranty do you provide?

Warranty coverage varies by product, manufacturer, and service. Ask for the applicable written warranty details before approving your project.

Do you provide free on-site measurements?

Professional measurement is part of planning many custom projects. Contact the team to confirm the visit scope and any applicable terms for your project.
